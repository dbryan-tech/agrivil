# SCREEN BEHAVIOR GUIDELINES

## Home
Must answer quickly:
- Where am I delivering to?
- What can I shop?
- What is fresh/recommended?
- What is local/nearby?
- What should I buy next?

## Product Details
Show:
- product photography
- name
- farmer
- rating
- price/unit
- quantity or weight
- availability
- relevant product information
- add-to-cart CTA

Variable-weight products must clearly distinguish:
- estimated weight
- price range
- final measured weight
- final price
- confirmation/reconciliation step

## Farmer Profile
Show:
- farm identity
- location
- trust indicators
- short story
- growing methods/values
- gallery
- products
- reviews

## Cart
Show:
- every selected item
- quantity/weight
- item price
- subtotal
- delivery fee
- total
- primary checkout action

## Checkout
Use a clear step sequence:
Address → Delivery time → Payment → Review → Confirmation

## Tracking
Prefer a clean timeline with:
- current status
- timestamps
- delivery estimate
- driver/partner details when available
- map only when useful

## Recipes
Recipe pages should connect content to commerce:
View recipe → view ingredients → add selected/all ingredients to cart

## Bundles / Subscriptions
Explain clearly:
- what is included
- cadence
- price
- savings/value
- next delivery
- manage/skip/cancel controls
