# AGRIVIL UX WRITING GUIDELINES

## Voice
Warm, clear, grounded, confident.

The brand should sound like a helpful local marketplace, not a financial institution or a tech startup.

## Prefer
"Fresh from local farmers."
"Delivering to KNUST, Kumasi"
"Choose a delivery time"
"Add to cart"
"Your order is on the way"
"From their soil to your kitchen"

## Avoid
"Revolutionary platform"
"Seamless ecosystem"
"Unlock your agricultural journey"
"Buy now!!!"
"Super easy!!!"

## Labels
Use sentence case.
Keep action labels short.

Good:
- Add to cart
- View farm
- Shop local
- Continue
- Place order
- Track order

Avoid:
- Continue Shopping Now
- Proceed With Your Purchase

## Error language
State:
1. what happened
2. what the user can do

Example:
"Payment didn't go through.
Try again or choose another payment method."

## Empty states
Never blame the user.
Offer one useful next action.

Example:
"No saved farmers yet.
Explore nearby farms to find producers you trust."

## Ghana context
Use:
- GH₵
- GhanaPostGPS
- local area names where appropriate
- MoMo
- delivery time slots
